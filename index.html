Marq-It Budget

Enter your passcode to open your budget. You only do this once on each phone.

Put it on your Home Screen first

  1. Tap ⋯ next to the address bar, then Share. On older iPhones, tap the Share button.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  3. Open Marq-It from your Home Screen and unlock it there.

The Home Screen app keeps its own copy of your sorting, so there is no need to unlock it here in Safari.

Put it on your Home Screen

  1. Tap Chrome’s menu ⋮ at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, then Install.
  3. Open Marq-It from your Home Screen.
